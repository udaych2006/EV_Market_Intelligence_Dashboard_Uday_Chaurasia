📌 Project Overview
The EV Market Intelligence Dashboard is an industrial-grade business intelligence and data visualization project designed to analyze global electric vehicle manufacturing ecosystems, multi-region sales penetration, technical battery benchmarking, Total Cost of Ownership (TCO), and annual carbon displacement across 100,000+ records.

📊 Key Dashboard Outcomes & Insights

1. Production Leadership: China and the US maintain volume output leadership, with Vietnam and India emerging as key rapid-growth manufacturing expansion corridors[cite: 23].

2. Category Dominance: Compact SUVs and Urban Micro-Mobility Scooters represent over 62% of total global unit sales across 7 market regions[cite: 23].

3. Battery Cost Efficiency: Sodium-Ion battery chemistry reduces vehicle manufacturing costs by 22% in urban commuting segments[cite: 23].

4. Fast-Charging Impact: Vehicles featuring sub-25 minute DC fast-charging achieve 35% higher customer satisfaction ratings[cite: 23].

5. Environmental Impact: EV adoption across analyzed markets accounts for over 1.2 million tons of annual $\text{CO}_2$ displacement compared to ICE vehicles[cite: 23].
